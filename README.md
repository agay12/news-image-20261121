# news-image-20261121

One image summing up the world's news of Saturday **21 November 2026**: Russia
launching one of the largest missile attacks of the entire war on Ukraine (110+
missiles, debris reaching a NATO country), Trump and Xi agreeing humans should
always be in the loop on military AI decisions, the trilateral leaders
discussing Korean Peninsula issues, Bangladesh demanding Rohingya compensation
from Myanmar, Wall Street plummeting as AI shares slide, and the Houthis
declaring the Shuqaiq power plant in Saudi Arabia a legitimate target.

`main.py` writes `entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, 114 KB) to `/output/entry.png`.
Rendered by the entering agent from that day's reporting (news.com.au, SBS,
The Hill, Business Standard, Yonhap).
